Beti Padhao, Beti Bachao

Bharat Vikas Parishad, through its "Beti Padhao, Beti Bachao" initiative, is committed to empowering girls and promoting gender equality in society.

The program focuses on encouraging education for girls, ensuring their safety, and creating awareness about their rights and importance in nation-building. Its primary aim is to eliminate gender discrimination and provide equal opportunities for girls to grow, learn, and succeed with dignity.

Progress & Social Development

"Beti Padhao, Beti Bachao" also emphasizes the importance of protecting the rights of girls and ensuring their well-being. By promoting self-confidence, the initiative helps girls become independent and responsible individuals.

It aims to build a society where daughters are respected, valued, and given equal opportunities in every field, contributing towards a balanced and progressive nation.

"It stands as a symbol of empowerment, equality, and a brighter future for the nation."

Bharat Vikas Parishad is a socio-cultural organization dedicated to the service of the nation through Seva, Sanskar, and Samarpan.

BVP Shivaji Shakha Moradabad

Bharat Vikas Parishad - Shivaji Shakha, Moradabad is a renowned social and cultural organization dedicated to the service of the nation and society. This branch was established in the year 2024, and since then it has been actively engaged in works for the welfare of society. With the vision of promoting Indian culture, values, and unity, this branch has been continuously contributing to nation-building. Shivaji Shakha, Moradabad is especially known for its social service, educational initiatives, and health programs. The branch works for the benefit of people from various sections of society and strives to improve their standard of living.

Mission & Vission

Caring for Society with a Pious Touch

Bharat Vikas Parishad - Shivaji Shakha, Moradabad is a renowned social and cultural organization dedicated to the service of the nation and society. Established with the vision of promoting Indian culture, values, and unity, we are actively engaged in works for the welfare of society through educational initiatives, health programs, and social service.

  • Serving society with compassion and equality.
  • Strengthening communities through service initiatives.
  • Promoting values, unity, and national pride.
  • Building a strong and value-based Bharat.
  • Growing Charitable Care Network
  • Inspiring future leaders with values and vision.
  • Commitment to Excellence.
  • Striving for the highest standards in service.
  • Upholding honesty and strong moral values.

Guru Vandan Chhatra Abhinandan

Bharat Vikas Parishad Initiative

Values for a Successful Life

"Guru Vandan Chhatra Abhinandan" is a value-based flagship programme of Bharat Vikas Parishad that aims to strengthen the cultural and moral foundation of students. It instills deep respect for parents, teachers, women, and elders, recognizing that their blessings play a vital role in shaping a successful life.

Under this initiative, representatives of the Parishad visit schools and colleges to express gratitude towards dedicated teachers and to felicitate meritorious students, creating a positive educational environment rooted in Sanatan values.

ESTD. 2001

The Sacred Bond

A unique feature of this programme is that students are encouraged to offer flowers to their teachers and seek their blessings by touching their feet (Charan Sparsh), nurturing humility and discipline in young minds.

Key Activities:
Moral Values Talk Oath Taking Teacher Felicitation Merit Recognition Principal Address

Honoring Excellence

Teachers and students are selected through a rigorous process to highlight the best of our educational community.

Criteria for Teachers:
Long Service Academic Excellence Co-curricular Success Prestigious Authorship
Criteria for Students:
Leadership Roles National Participation Sports Talent Cultural Excellence

Bharat Vikas Parishad

Samajik Pariyojana

Saralta aur Sanskaar ki Pehchan

"Bharat Vikas Parishad ka ek mahatvapurn samajik pariyojana hai jiska uddeshya samaj mein saralta, samanta aur sahayog ki bhavna ko badhava dena hai."

Is karyakram ke madhyam se arthik roop se kamzor parivaron ke ladka-ladkiyon ke vivaah ko bina kisi dikhave aur adhik kharch ke saral tareeke se sampann karaya jata hai. Iska mukhya lakshya dahej pratha aur vyarth kharch ko kam karna hai.

Samajik Sandesh

One Platform, One People

Yah karyakram bina kisi bhedbhaav ke sabhi vargon ke logon ko ek manch par laata hai. **Yah samaj ko ek sandesh deta hai ki vivaah jaise sanskaar mein adhik dikhava ya kharch ki koi aavashyakta nahi hai, balki saralta aur sanskar hi iski asli pehchan hai.**

Isse na keval vivaah ka kharch kam hota hai balki samaj mein ekta aur sahayog ki bhavna bhi majboot hoti hai.

Project ke Char Stambh

Principles that Foster Social Justice and Equality

Saralta

Bina kisi dikhave ke pavitra bandhan ko sampann karana hi mukhya uddeshya hai.

Samanta

Sabhi vargon ke logon ko ek hi manch par laakar bhedbhaav ko khatm karna.

Sanskaar

Vivaah ko ek gauravmay aur maryadit avsar banaye rakhna.

Sahayog

Samaj ke sevabhavi log milkar vivaah ki poori vyavastha karte hain.

Poori Vyavastha, Ek Saath

Parishad ke sadasya milkar vivaah ki poori vyavastha karte hain taaki nav-vivaahit jode apna naya jeevan bina kisi arthik bojh ke shuru kar sakein.

Bharat Vikas Parishad

Bharat Ko Jano

Nation-Wide Quiz Program

Know Your Nation, Love Your Heritage

A Bharat Ko Jano is an important project of Bharat Vikas Parishad aimed at creating awareness among students about India's rich heritage, culture, history, and national values. The main objective is to develop a sense of patriotism and pride in the younger generation.

Through this competition, students learn about India's freedom struggle, great leaders, geographical diversity, and achievements in various fields. The program is conducted at branch, state, regional, and national levels.

Bharat Ko Jano

It motivates students to go beyond textbooks and explore real facts about the nation, its traditions, and its development journey. By encouraging students to explore and appreciate India's cultural and historical richness, this program contributes towards building a strong, aware, and value-based society.

The competition creates a healthy spirit of learning among participants, pushing them to perform better and gain deeper knowledge about the sacrifices made by freedom fighters and the contributions of great personalities.

Shaping Informed Citizens

Developing analytical skills and curiosity through competitive learning.

Knowledge & Identity

Enhances general knowledge while shaping informed citizens who understand the importance of unity in diversity.

Confidence & Skills

Improves thinking ability, communication skills, and self-confidence through interactive competition formats.

Emotional Bonding

Strengthens the bond with the nation by teaching respect for diversity and the value of unity among communities.

Bharat Vikas Parishad is a socio-cultural organization dedicated to the service of the nation through Seva, Sanskar, and Samarpan.
